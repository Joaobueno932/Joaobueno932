## About me

I'm **João Lucas Pinheiro**, a Computer Engineer and software developer based in **Campo Grande, Brazil**. I work across backend and full-stack development, focusing on **secure APIs, scalable architectures, data integration, automation and AI-powered applications**.

My professional experience includes building solutions for operational management, digital services, energy and sustainability. I enjoy connecting solid software engineering practices with practical business problems.

- **Core focus:** Backend engineering, APIs, system integration, architecture and full-stack products

- **Engineering mindset:** Maintainability, security, observability and measurable product value
